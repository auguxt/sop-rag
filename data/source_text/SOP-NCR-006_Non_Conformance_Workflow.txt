Title: Non-Conformance Workflow
Document Type: Standard Operating Procedure
Revision: C
Effective Date: 2026-01-05
Owner: Quality Engineering

1. Purpose
This procedure defines how nonconforming product is identified, documented, reviewed and dispositioned.

2. Scope
Applies to nonconformances found at incoming, in-process and final inspection and to customer returns.

3. Responsibilities
- Person finding the defect: segregates the product and raises the NCR.
- Quality Engineer: investigates the root cause and coordinates the Material Review Board (MRB).
- Material Review Board (Quality Manager, Production Manager and Design Engineer): decides the disposition.

4. Workflow Steps
4.1 Segregate the nonconforming product and attach a red HOLD tag.
4.2 Raise an NCR on form QF-601 with part number, batch, quantity, defect code (REF-DEF-005) and photographs.
4.3 Notify the Quality Engineer and the area supervisor within 24 hours.
4.4 The Quality Engineer reviews the NCR and convenes the MRB within 3 working days.
4.5 The MRB decides the disposition: use-as-is, rework, return to supplier or scrap.
4.6 For recurring or Major defects, open a Corrective Action Request (CAR) and perform a 5-Why root cause analysis.
4.7 Close the NCR only after the disposition has been completed and verified.

5. Notification Matrix
- Incoming material defect: inform the Incoming Quality Lead and the Supplier Quality Engineer.
- In-process defect: inform the Production Supervisor and the Line Quality Inspector.
- Final inspection defect: inform the Production Supervisor and the Quality Manager.
- Critical defect at any stage: inform the Quality Manager and the Plant Head immediately.

6. Acceptable Evidence
- Completed QF-601 form with defect photographs.
- MRB disposition record with signatures.
- CAR and root cause analysis for Major defects.

7. Records
- NCR records are retained for 5 years.
