Document ID: SOP-QA-001
Title: Incoming Material Inspection
Document Type: Standard Operating Procedure
Revision: C
Effective Date: 2026-03-01
Owner: Quality Assurance Department

1. Purpose
This procedure defines how purchased raw material, castings and bought-out components are inspected on receipt before they are moved to stores or used in production.

2. Scope
Applies to all supplier deliveries received at the goods-receiving area, including bar stock, aluminium castings, bearings, seals and packaging material. Customer-supplied material is also covered.

3. Responsibilities
- Incoming Inspector: performs the inspection and completes form QF-101.
- Incoming Quality Lead: reviews rejected lots and signs the inspection report.
- Supplier Quality Engineer: communicates rejections to the supplier and tracks corrective action.
- Stores: moves only lots tagged ACCEPTED into stock.

4. Sampling Plan
- Sampling follows ISO 2859-1, General Inspection Level II, AQL 1.0 for major defects.
- Lots of 50 units or fewer are inspected 100 percent.
- The first delivery from a new supplier receives 100 percent dimensional inspection.

5. Inspection Steps
5.1 Match the supplier delivery note with the purchase order: part number, revision and quantity.
5.2 Check that a Certificate of Conformance (CoC) and, for metals, a material test certificate is supplied for each heat or lot.
5.3 Inspect packaging for damage or moisture and confirm each package carries a supplier label (see SOP-LBL-008).
5.4 Carry out visual inspection of sampled parts for scratches, dents, corrosion and burrs using the defect codes in REF-DEF-005.
5.5 Measure the critical dimensions listed on the drawing for each sampled part.
5.6 Before measuring, confirm the instrument carries a valid calibration label (see SOP-CAL-004).
5.7 Record all results and the instrument ID on Incoming Inspection Report QF-101.
5.8 Apply a green ACCEPTED tag to conforming lots or a red HOLD tag to suspect lots and move HOLD lots to the quarantine rack.

6. Acceptable Evidence
- Supplier Certificate of Conformance and material test certificate.
- Completed and signed form QF-101 with instrument IDs.
- Measurement data sheet for the sampled parts.
- Dated photographs of any defect found.

7. Defect Handling and Escalation
- Place the whole lot on HOLD; do not return it to the supplier without a disposition.
- Raise a Non-Conformance Report (NCR) as per SOP-NCR-006 within 24 hours of detection.
- Inform the Incoming Quality Lead and the Supplier Quality Engineer; the Supplier Quality Engineer notifies the supplier.

8. Records
- QF-101 forms and supplier certificates are retained for 3 years in the quality records room.
