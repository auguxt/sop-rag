Document ID: CHK-MC-007
Title: CNC Machine Quality Checklist
Document Type: Checklist
Revision: A
Effective Date: 2025-10-20
Owner: Production Engineering

1. Purpose
Daily checklist for CNC operators to confirm that the machine and gauges are fit to produce conforming parts.

2. Start-of-Shift Checks
- Coolant level and concentration checked and recorded.
- Machine warm-up cycle completed.
- Work-holding fixture clean and free of chips.
- Correct program number and revision loaded as per the traveler.

3. Gauge Checks
- Vernier calipers used at the machine shall be calibrated every 12 months.
- Check the calibration label date before use; do not use a gauge past its due date.
- Zero-check calipers and micrometers at the start of every shift.

4. First-Piece and Patrol Checks
- Perform the first-piece inspection as per SOP-QA-003 and obtain Line Quality Inspector sign-off.
- Perform patrol checks every 2 hours and record them on QF-301.
- Check surfaces for scratches caused by chips or handling.

5. End-of-Shift Checks
- Clean the machine and the fixture.
- Hand over open issues and HOLD parts to the next shift.

6. Escalation
- If a dimension is out of tolerance or a gauge has failed, stop the machine and inform the Production Supervisor.
