Document ID: SOP-CAL-004
Title: Calibration of Measuring Instruments
Document Type: Standard Operating Procedure
Revision: E
Effective Date: 2026-02-10
Owner: Metrology Laboratory

1. Purpose
This procedure ensures that all measuring and test equipment used for product acceptance is calibrated, identified and traceable to national standards.

2. Scope
Covers vernier calipers, micrometers, height gauges, pin gauges, dial indicators and the coordinate measuring machine (CMM).

3. Responsibilities
- Metrology Technician: maintains the calibration schedule and performs in-house calibration.
- Instrument User: checks the calibration label before every use.
- Quality Manager: selects and authorises external calibration laboratories.

4. Calibration Intervals
- Vernier calipers and micrometers: calibrate every 6 months.
- Height gauges, dial indicators and pin gauges: calibrate every 12 months.
- CMM: calibrate every 12 months through an NABL-accredited external laboratory.

5. Calibration Procedure
5.1 Clean the instrument and allow it to stabilise in the metrology room for at least 2 hours.
5.2 Check the instrument (for example a vernier caliper, micrometer or height gauge) against reference gauge blocks at a minimum of 3 points across its range.
5.3 Record the observed error at each point on Calibration Record QF-401.
5.4 Compare the observed error with the permissible error stated in the instrument master list.
5.5 If the error is within the permissible error, attach a green calibration label showing the instrument ID and next due date.
5.6 If the error is outside the permissible error, follow Section 6 (Failed Calibration Handling).

6. Failed Calibration Handling
6.1 Attach a red OUT OF CALIBRATION - DO NOT USE tag and remove the instrument from the shop floor.
6.2 Inform the Metrology Technician and the Quality Manager on the same day.
6.3 Use the instrument ID to identify all product measured with it since its last successful calibration.
6.4 Carry out an impact assessment and re-inspect the suspect product with a calibrated instrument.
6.5 Raise an NCR as per SOP-NCR-006 if suspect product has already been accepted or shipped.
6.6 Repair, adjust or scrap the instrument and recalibrate it before it is returned to service.

7. Acceptable Evidence
- Calibration certificate or QF-401 record with traceability to national standards.
- Valid green calibration label on the instrument.
- Impact assessment record for every failed calibration.

8. Records
- Calibration records are retained for the life of the instrument plus 1 year.
