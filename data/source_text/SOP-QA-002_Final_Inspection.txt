Title: Final Inspection
Document Type: Standard Operating Procedure
Revision: D
Effective Date: 2026-03-01
Owner: Quality Assurance Department

1. Purpose
This procedure defines the final inspection of finished products before packing and before the batch is presented for release.

2. Scope
Applies to all finished machined and assembled products manufactured at the plant, including rework batches.

3. Responsibilities
- Final Inspector: performs the final inspection and completes form QF-201.
- Production Supervisor: provides the work order traveler and segregates rejected product.
- Quality Manager: reviews the inspection package and is the only role authorised to release a batch (see SOP-REL-010).

4. Sampling Plan
- Sampling follows ISO 2859-1, General Inspection Level II, AQL 0.65 for major defects.
- Critical-to-quality (CTQ) dimensions are checked on 100 percent of parts.
- The first batch after a new setup or engineering change receives a full first article inspection.

5. Inspection Steps
5.1 Confirm that all in-process inspection records (SOP-QA-003) for the batch are complete and signed.
5.2 Verify part number, drawing revision and quantity against the work order traveler.
5.3 Carry out visual inspection of all surfaces under minimum 1000 lux lighting as per SOP-SURF-009.
5.4 Measure all CTQ dimensions; use the CMM for dimensions marked CMM on the drawing.
5.5 Perform the functional or fit check where the drawing specifies one.
5.6 Verify the product label, batch number and barcode against the traveler (see SOP-LBL-008).
5.7 Record all results on Final Inspection Report QF-201.
5.8 Submit the completed inspection package to the Quality Manager for the release decision. The Final Inspector does not release batches.

6. Acceptable Evidence
- Completed and signed form QF-201.
- CMM report for the CTQ dimensions.
- Label verification checklist with the barcode scan result.
- Work order traveler with all operation sign-offs.
- Dated photographs of any defect found.

7. Defect Handling and Escalation
- Stop packing the batch and move suspect product to the red quarantine bin.
- Raise an NCR as per SOP-NCR-006 before the end of the shift.
- Inform the Production Supervisor and the Quality Manager immediately.

8. Records
- QF-201 forms, CMM reports and travelers are retained for 5 years as required by customer contracts.
