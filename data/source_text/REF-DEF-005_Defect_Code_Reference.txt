Title: Defect Code Reference

1. Purpose
This reference provides standard defect codes and classifications so that defects are recorded consistently on inspection reports and NCRs.

2. Defect Classification
- Critical: defect likely to cause an unsafe condition or complete loss of function.
- Major: defect likely to cause failure or to materially reduce usability.
- Minor: defect unlikely to reduce usability, typically cosmetic.

3. Defect Codes
- SC-01 Scratch: line mark, scuff or abrasion on the surface. Minor on Class B surfaces; Major on Class A surfaces when outside the cosmetic limits.
- DM-02 Dimensional mismatch: dimension outside the drawing tolerance (oversize or undersize). Major; Critical when the dimension is CTQ.
- LB-03 Missing or incorrect label: package or part without the required label, or label data that does not match the traveler. Major.
- CF-04 Failed calibration: inspection performed with an instrument that is out of calibration or overdue. Major (system defect).
- DN-05 Dent: local deformation caused by impact. Minor or Major depending on location.
- CR-06 Corrosion: rust or oxidation on the surface. Major.
- BR-07 Burr: raised sharp edge left by machining. Minor unless it is on a sealing or mating face.

4. Cosmetic Acceptance Limits
- Scratch on Class A (visible) surface: maximum length 5 mm, maximum width 0.2 mm.
- Class B (hidden) surfaces: scratches are acceptable provided they do not expose base material or affect function.
- Dents on Class A surfaces are not acceptable.

5. Disposition Guidance
- Record the defect code on the inspection report and on the NCR.
- Disposition options are use-as-is, rework, return to supplier or scrap, decided through SOP-NCR-006.
- Use-as-is for a Major defect requires a customer concession.
