Document ID: SOP-REL-010
Title: Batch Release and Quarantine Control
Document Type: Standard Operating Procedure
Revision: C
Effective Date: 2026-03-15
Owner: Quality Manager

1. Purpose
This procedure defines who may release finished batches for dispatch and how quarantined product is controlled.

2. Release Authority
- Only the Quality Manager, or a deputy named in writing by the Quality Manager, may approve the release of a batch.
- Inspectors, operators and software tools, including any digital assistant, cannot approve or release batches.

3. Release Prerequisites
3.1 Final Inspection Report QF-201 is complete and signed.
3.2 All NCRs for the batch are closed or have an approved MRB disposition.
3.3 Product labels and quantities have been verified.
3.4 Customer-specific documents, such as a certificate of conformance, are prepared.

4. Release Steps
4.1 The Final Inspector submits the inspection package to the Quality Manager.
4.2 The Quality Manager reviews the package against the release prerequisites.
4.3 If acceptable, the Quality Manager signs the release on form QF-701 and the batch is tagged RELEASED.
4.4 If not acceptable, the batch remains on HOLD and the reason is recorded on QF-701.

5. Quarantine Control
- HOLD product is kept in the locked quarantine area with red tags.
- Only the Quality Manager may authorise removal of product from quarantine.

6. Records
- Release records on form QF-701 are retained for 5 years.
