Document ID: SOP-LBL-008
Title: Labelling and Traceability
Document Type: Standard Operating Procedure
Revision: B
Effective Date: 2025-12-01
Owner: Stores and Quality Assurance

1. Purpose
This procedure ensures that material and products are correctly identified and traceable through all stages of manufacture.

2. Label Requirements
- Every package must carry a label showing part number, revision, batch number, quantity and barcode.
- Finished product labels must also show the customer part number and date of manufacture.

3. Label Verification Steps
3.1 Check that a label is present on every package or bin.
3.2 Scan the barcode and compare the decoded data with the traveler or delivery note.
3.3 Confirm that the label is legible and securely attached.
3.4 Record the result on the label verification checklist.

4. Missing or Incorrect Label
4.1 If a label is missing or incorrect, stop the movement of the package and tag it HOLD (defect code LB-03).
4.2 Do not create a replacement label from memory; re-identify the material only from the traveler, the delivery note or by re-measurement.
4.3 Inform the Stores In-charge and the Line Quality Inspector.
4.4 Raise an NCR as per SOP-NCR-006 if the identity of the material cannot be confirmed.

5. Records
- Label verification checklists are filed with the related inspection report.
