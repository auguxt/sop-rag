Document ID: SOP-QA-003
Title: In-Process Inspection
Document Type: Standard Operating Procedure
Revision: B
Effective Date: 2026-01-15
Owner: Quality Assurance Department

1. Purpose
This procedure defines the inspections carried out at the machine during production so that problems are detected before a full batch is made.

2. Scope
Applies to CNC turning, milling and grinding operations.

3. Responsibilities
- Machine Operator: performs first-piece and patrol checks.
- Line Quality Inspector: signs off the first-piece inspection before production continues.
- Production Supervisor: stops the machine when a dimension is out of tolerance.

4. Inspection Frequency
- First-piece inspection at every setup, tool change or program change.
- Patrol inspection every 2 hours during production.
- Last-piece inspection at the end of each batch.

5. Inspection Steps
5.1 Measure all drawing dimensions on the first piece and record them on In-Process Check Sheet QF-301.
5.2 Obtain sign-off from the Line Quality Inspector before running the batch.
5.3 During patrol inspection, measure the dimensions marked with a patrol symbol on the control plan.
5.4 If a dimension is out of tolerance (dimensional mismatch, code DM-02), stop the machine immediately.
5.5 Segregate all parts produced since the last good patrol check and tag them HOLD.
5.6 Correct the cause (tool offset, worn insert or fixture) and repeat the first-piece inspection.

6. Acceptable Evidence
- Completed QF-301 check sheet with operator and inspector initials.
- Signed first-piece inspection record.
- Tool change log entries.

7. Defect Handling and Escalation
- Inform the Production Supervisor and the Line Quality Inspector immediately when a dimensional mismatch is found.
- Raise an NCR as per SOP-NCR-006 if any suspect parts are confirmed nonconforming.

8. Records
- QF-301 check sheets are retained for 3 years.
