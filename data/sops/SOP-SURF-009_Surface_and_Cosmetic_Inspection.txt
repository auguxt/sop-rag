Document ID: SOP-SURF-009
Title: Surface Finish and Cosmetic Inspection
Document Type: Standard Operating Procedure
Revision: B
Effective Date: 2026-04-01
Owner: Quality Assurance Department

1. Purpose
This procedure defines how visual and cosmetic inspection of machined and painted surfaces is performed.

2. Scope
Covers all Class A surfaces (visible to the end customer) and Class B surfaces (hidden after assembly).

3. Inspection Conditions
- Lighting: minimum 1000 lux at the inspection surface.
- Viewing distance: 450 mm, holding each surface in view for about 10 seconds.
- Inspect without magnification unless the drawing specifies otherwise.

4. Inspection Steps
4.1 Clean the part with a lint-free cloth.
4.2 View each Class A surface at the specified distance, rotating the part under the light.
4.3 Run a fingernail across any scratch; a scratch that catches the fingernail is rejected regardless of its length.
4.4 Mark defects with a removable marker and record the defect code (REF-DEF-005) on the inspection report.
4.5 Segregate rejected parts and tag them HOLD.

5. Acceptance Criteria
- Scratch on Class A (visible) surface: maximum length 3 mm, maximum width 0.2 mm.
- No more than 2 scratches are allowed on a single Class A surface.
- Class B surfaces: scratches are acceptable if they do not expose base material.

6. Escalation
- Inform the Line Quality Inspector when scratches repeat on consecutive parts, because this indicates a handling or fixture problem.
- Raise an NCR as per SOP-NCR-006 for every rejected batch.
